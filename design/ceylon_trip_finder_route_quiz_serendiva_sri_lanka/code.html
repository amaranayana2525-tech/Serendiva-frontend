Common Route Atelier Inquiries

Can we adjust the route after submitting the quiz?

Yes, completely. This quiz is a creative starting blueprint. Dinesh will converse with you on WhatsApp or email to refine stopover durations, add bespoke birdwatching detours, or switch lodging styles.

Who handles our transport throughout Sri Lanka?

You will be accompanied by Dinesh Jayasuriya or one of Serendiva’s hand-selected National Chauffeur Lecturers in a sanitized, climate-controlled executive SUV or micro-van with bottled Ceylon water and mobile Wi-Fi.

Are train tickets guaranteed?

Yes. Unlike standard mass agencies, Serendiva secures official SLGR first-class observation salon tickets directly through authorized governmental reservation systems 30 days prior to travel.

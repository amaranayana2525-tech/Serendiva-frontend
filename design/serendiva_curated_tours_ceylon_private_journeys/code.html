Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our private chauffeur-guided Ceylon journeys.

tune

Can we modify any listed itinerary?

Absolutely. All itineraries shown are curated master-frameworks. We regularly swap accommodations for your preferred boutique tea bungalows or ocean villas, add layover rest days, or weave in unique regional curiosities.

directions_car

What vehicles are used for private tours?

We maintain a dedicated fleet of modern, air-conditioned Toyota luxury vans and SUVs. Each vehicle features panoramic high-visibility tinted windows, complimentary Wi-Fi, chilled king coconut refreshments, and generous luggage stowage.

confirmation_number

Are entrance tickets and permits included?

Yes. All UNESCO Cultural Triangle passes, wildlife sanctuary fees, scenic railway tickets, and private 4x4 safaris are pre-arranged and included in your tailored quote, eliminating queue delays throughout your journey.

handshake

How do we confirm our booking?

Once you and Dinesh finalize the day-by-day plan, we reserve your selected villas and vehicle. We operate an unhurried, transparent concierge process with direct wire transfers and zero hidden add-ons.

## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an evocative, cinematic journey through Sri Lanka, balancing the poise of private boutique hospitality with bespoke, handcrafted authenticity. The visual tone speaks to culturally discerning travelers seeking tailored itineraries, architectural villas, endemic wildlife, and colonial-heritage estates.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges warm editorial elegance and contemporary minimalism. Generous negative space, asymmetric layouts, and gentle layered framing echo high-end architectural monographs. Interactions are deliberate, graceful, and unhurried—favoring soft tactile shifts over abrupt digital cues.

## Colors
The palette evokes the natural and cultural landscape of Sri Lanka—from the Indian Ocean and cinnamon hills to terraced tea estates.

- **Deep Ocean Teal (`#0F4C5C`)**: The sovereign primary tone. Anchors key interactive moments, brand accents, and authoritative focal points.
- **Warm Sand (`#F7F1E6`)**: The ambient background canvas. Provides a soft, paper-like warmth that prevents clinical sterile contrast.
- **Tea Green (`#5B7F4F`)**: Used for botanical accents, nature-bound badges, wellness itineraries, and restorative visual pauses.
- **Cinnamon (`#C4623A`)**: The kinetic secondary tone. Applied to seasonal highlights, exclusive calls to action, and route tags.
- **Soft Gold (`#C9A24B`)**: Reserved for verified license seals, concierge status, private transport tiers, and fine micro-borders.
- **Charcoal (`#2B2B2B`)**: The primary text color, delivering editorial contrast while avoiding harsh absolute blacks.
- **Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Utilized for foreground cards, image mounts, floating overlays, and high-readability containers.

## Typography
Typographic scale mirrors the cadence of a curated travel book. 

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Expressive, transitional serifs provide cinematic grandeur. Use italicized treatments sparingly for location highlights, quote attributions, and local botanical terms.
- **Body & UI Controls (`Inter`)**: High-legibility grotesque sans-serif sets clean, airy, neutral body text. Provides pristine contrast against the expressive serifs.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase treatments with generous letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to create understated architectural markers for tags, categories, and tour statistics.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout breaks rigid uniformity through an asymmetrical 12-column system, creating an organic rhythm of staggered image cards, overlapping caption blocks, and wide breathing zones.

### Breakpoints & Flow
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Overlapping components stack into linear, staggered card cascades with full-width photography bleeding into margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Introduces paired editorial spreads where pull quotes tuck into image indentations.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, max-width `1440px`, `margin: 5rem`, `gutter: 2.5rem`. Enables asymmetrical offsets, such as text spanning columns 2–6 paired with an offset photography frame spanning columns 6–12 with negative vertical overlap (`-2rem` to `-4rem`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is realized through soft, ambient light mimicking tropical morning haze rather than harsh digital drop shadows. 

- **Organic Ambient Elevation**: Shadows utilize Warm Sand undertones mixed with muted Charcoal: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -4px rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Card Stacking & Negative Overlap**: Floating editorial containers overlap background photography by `1.5rem` to `3rem`, separated by crisp `1px` borders tinted with Soft Gold (`rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.25)`) or pure Crisp White surfaces.
- **Modals & Drawers**: Backdrop dimming relies on Deep Ocean Teal mixed at 40% opacity with a subtle `8px` Gaussian blur, lending a sophisticated, immersed feel to booking flows.

## Shapes
A baseline curvature of `12px` (`0.75rem`) sets a warm, hand-finished aesthetic without feeling overly playful or circular.

- **Primary Interactive Elements**: Fixed at `12px` border radius (`rounded-lg`), offering a balanced, tactile shape for buttons, input fields, and standard cards.
- **Pills**: Badges, status markers, and day-by-day itinerary pills use full pill shaping (`9999px`) to cleanly demarcate micro-data.
- **Image Frames**: Curated editorial photographs employ unified `12px` rounding, occasionally contrasted with an asymmetric single-corner arch (`top-left: 48px`, `top-right: 12px`, `bottom-right: 12px`, `bottom-left: 12px`) for featured heritage property profiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Ocean Teal background, Crisp White label in `label-lg`, `12px` radius, padding `1rem 2rem`. Subtle hover elevation and slight color shift to `#0A3540`.
- **Secondary (Bespoke Inquiry)**: Transparent surface with a `1.5px` border in Deep Ocean Teal or Soft Gold, text in Deep Ocean Teal. Hover transitions into Warm Sand fill.
- **Cinnamon Accent**: Used exclusively for direct transactional hooks (e.g., "Reserve This Expedition"), featuring `#C4623A` background and Crisp White text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Badges**: Pill-shaped containers (`9999px`) with Tea Green background at 15% opacity, filled with dark Tea Green text (`#5B7F4F`) in `label-md`.
- **License Seals**: Soft Gold outline (`1px`), Warm Sand surface, paired with an authentic guide verification icon.

### Form Inputs
- Background in Crisp White, border `1px` solid `rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.15)`, radius `12px`, padding `1rem 1.25rem`. 
- Focus state reveals a `1.5px` ring in Deep Ocean Teal without loud neon outer glows. Helper text and floating labels set in Inter `body-md`.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Itinerary Card**: Background Crisp White, `12px` radius, subtle organic shadow. Image occupies top 60% with an overlaid pill badge. Content section features a Playfair Display heading, Inter body snippet, and travel logistics metadata (duration, elevation, private chauffeur tags) aligned along a segmented grid.
- **Overlapping Quotation / Note Module**: Floats halfway across image boundaries with a Crisp White canvas, Soft Gold left accent line (`3px`), and `Playfair Display` italic text.

### Lists & Timeline Itineraries
- Connected vertical nodes using a `1px` Warm Sand to Tea Green progressive timeline stroke. 
- Node indicators are styled as dual concentric rings (outer Warm Sand, inner Deep Ocean Teal).
